Privacy Policy

GlitchIt — Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how GlitchIt ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information about you when you use our social marketplace app and website (collectively, the "Service"). By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address, username, password (stored securely, hashed), and profile picture when you sign up or edit your profile.
  • Content you create: videos ("Glitches"), photos, stories, captions, comments, likes, saves, shares, direct messages, and live audio/video call participation.
  • Usage information: pages you visit, features you use, and interactions such as follows, searches, and interests you select at signup.
  • Device & technical information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and log data when you access the Service.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve the Service, including: creating and managing your account; displaying content, stories, and reels in your feed; enabling messaging and group calls; processing likes, comments, shares, and saves; personalizing your experience; providing customer support and the AI assistant; detecting abuse and ensuring the security of the Service; and complying with legal obligations.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences (such as theme and interests), and understand how the Service is used. Third-party advertising partners, including Google, may also use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some parts of the Service may not function properly without them.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Ad Partners (Google AdSense)

The Service displays advertising served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn more about opting out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To understand how Google uses data when you use our site, see How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Advertisers receive aggregated or anonymized reporting only.

6. Third-Party Services

To operate the Service we rely on trusted providers, who process data only as needed to provide their services to us: Supabase (authentication, database, and media storage), Cloudinary (profile picture delivery), LiveKit (live audio/video calls), NVIDIA (AI assistant responses), and Deezer / Apple Music (music previews in the composer). Each provider's own privacy policy governs their handling of the data we entrust to them.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account information and content for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Stories expire automatically after 24 hours. When you delete your account or content, we take reasonable steps to remove it from active use.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, update, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of personalized advertising. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. California residents may also request information about the categories of personal information we disclose.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted transmission and securely hashed passwords. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, contact us at support@glitchit.app. We may update this policy from time to time; changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
