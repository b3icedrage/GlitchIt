About GlitchIt

The social marketplace for short-form video — where everyday little moments become content, community and commerce.

What is GlitchIt?

GlitchIt is a social marketplace where creators share short glitch-style videos, photos, stories and “drops,” and followers can engage with that content and shop creator storefronts. It combines the feel of a short-video community with a marketplace — creators build an audience with their content and can earn by selling drops directly to the people who follow them.

Everything happens in one app: post a glitch video, share a 24-hour story, go live, chat in real time, and shop — without jumping between platforms.

What you can do on GlitchIt

  • Share Glitches. Post short, punchy video clips with music, captions and effects. Followers can like, comment, save and share them.
  • Post stories. Share moments that disappear after 24 hours, with music previews, location notes and swipe navigation.
  • Create AI glitch videos. Describe the clip you want in the camera page’s AI Glitch studio, pick a model such as Veo, Kling or LTX-Video, and the clip renders for you to preview, download or share.
  • Go live and call. Host live broadcasts and group audio/video calls, and chat with your community in real time.
  • Shop drops. Browse creator storefronts and buy drops — merch, art, digital goods and finds — directly through the marketplace checkout.
  • Chat and connect. Send direct messages, join channels around your interests, and follow the creators you love.

How it works

  1. Create. Record a glitch video, story or drop with the in-app camera, pick your music, and add effects.
  2. Share. Post to your feed, go live, or send a story — your followers see it instantly and can react, comment and share.
  3. Earn. Sell drops from your storefront and grow into a professional account to unlock the creator earnings dashboard.

Community guidelines

GlitchIt is built on respect. We ask everyone to:

  • Post original content and respect copyright, including music and media.
  • Treat other people with kindness — no harassment, hate speech, bullying or threats.
  • Keep the platform safe: no spam, scams, misleading content, or anything illegal.
  • Respect privacy — don’t share other people’s personal information without consent.
  • Report content that breaks these guidelines so we can review it.

Accounts that violate these guidelines may have content removed or access restricted. If you see something that concerns you, contact us at support@glitchit.app.

How creators earn

Creators on GlitchIt can earn in two ways:

  • Storefront drops. List items — merch, digital goods or finds — and keep the proceeds from sales through the marketplace checkout.
  • Professional creator earnings. Switch to a professional account and work toward the eligibility milestones (100K followers, 5,000 watch hours and 500K views) to unlock the creator earnings dashboard.
