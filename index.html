What is GlitchIt?

GlitchIt is a social marketplace for short-form video. Creators share glitch-style videos, stories, and drops, while followers engage with content, go live, and shop creator storefronts — all in one place.

🎬 Share Glitches

Post short punchy video clips with music, captions and effects. Your followers can like, comment, save and share them.

📖 Stories

Share moments that disappear after 24 hours, with music, location notes and swipe navigation.

🛒 Shop Drops

Browse creator storefronts and buy drops — merch, art, digital goods — directly through the marketplace.

📡 Go Live

Host live broadcasts and group calls. Chat with your community in real time.

Creators on GlitchIt can earn by selling drops from their storefronts and growing into professional accounts to unlock the creator earnings dashboard. Learn more about GlitchIt →
